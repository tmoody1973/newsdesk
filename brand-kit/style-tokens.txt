editorial mixed-media collage. GROUND: warm cream paper with visible halftone dot texture and paper grain — the background is always warm cream, never blue, never tan, never white. ACCENTS: deep navy and coral only, used as flat torn-paper shapes; no other accent colour appears. ELEMENTS: archival photo cutouts with rough torn white paper borders, tape strips, hand-drawn black marker circles and arrows. Non-photorealistic, no live-action.
